# What travel does not do, and what it only defers

- **Never multi-user.** The owner is the only user, so there is never a second
  identity, a login or per-person data; see the Cloudflare Access decision.
  The one opening that stays is read-only: other people may some day be
  allowed to view chosen pages or trips. Nobody else ever edits.
- **Expenses are deferred, not ruled out.** A leg carries a `price` so a
  ticket can be compared and recorded, and nothing adds those up into a trip
  cost. The owner may want expense tracking far in the future, so don't build
  it now, and don't design anything that would make adding it later harder.
